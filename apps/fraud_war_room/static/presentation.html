01 / THE PROBLEM

Fraud teams see the alert.
They still need the story.

Modern fraud is coordinated, adaptive, and expensive to explain after the fact. A single score cannot carry the investigation.

01

Score without context

Rules and models flag anomalies, but investigators must reconstruct the customer, device, beneficiary, sequence, and network story by hand.

↗
02

Controls without a sparring partner

A control may stop fraud while creating false positives, customer friction, or a new bypass. Most workflows discover that too late.

↗
03

Decisions without a defensible trail

Evidence, dissent, approval, and outcomes live across tools. The audit question arrives after the decision.

↗
THE GAP
1 → ∞
